Delete your account

Last updated: November 25, 2026

How to delete your Rayita account and your data.

From the app (fastest)

  1. Sign in to Rayita.
  2. On the main screen, open the Account menu (the person icon, bottom right).
  3. Choose Delete account.
  4. Type your account's email to confirm and tap Delete forever.

Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone.

If you can't get into the app

Write to support@alanbazan.com.mx with the subject "Delete account", from the email you signed up with. We delete your account and data within 30 days at most and confirm by email.

What is deleted

  • Your account (name, email and, if you signed in with Google, your Google account identifier).
  • All your habits and daily marks.
  • The record of your "Remove ads" purchase on our server (Google Play keeps its own order history; refunds are requested from Google Play).
  • Your open sessions on all your devices.

What may be kept for a while

Technical server logs (IP address, date and request) and the hosting provider's backups may be kept for a limited time before they are erased. Outside those copies we do not keep your habits or marks in a form we can read.

If you signed in with Google

Deleting your Rayita account does not delete your Google account. If you wish, you can also remove Rayita's access from your Google account settings, under "Security → Third-party apps with account access".

More information

See the privacy policy or write to support@alanbazan.com.mx.
