Privacy policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

What data Rayita stores, what it is used for and how you can control it.

1. Who is responsible

Rayita is a habit-tracking app operated by Alan Bazán ("we"). For any privacy matter write to rayita@alanbazan.com.mx.

2. What data we collect

  • Account: your name, email and password. The password is stored as a hash, never in plain text, and we cannot read it.
  • If you sign in with Google: your name, email and Google account identifier. We never receive your Google password.
  • Your content: the habits you create (name, color, icon, goals and active days) and your daily marks (date and status).
  • Email verification: a 6-digit code stored as a hash that expires after 15 minutes.
  • Technical data: a session token stored on your device or browser, the language you choose, and server logs (IP address, date and request) generated by the hosting provider for security and diagnostics.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, microphone, camera, health data or payment data. We do not use advertising or third-party analytics tools.

3. How we use it

  • To create and maintain your account and sign you in.
  • To show your habits, marks, streaks and goals.
  • To verify your email and prevent abuse or unauthorized access.
  • To answer the messages you send us.

4. Who we share it with

We do not sell or give away your data. These providers are involved only to run the service:

  • Hosting (HostGator): hosts the API and database and sends verification emails.
  • Google: if you choose to sign in with Google, Google authenticates you. The app also downloads the Nunito typeface from Google Fonts, which may reveal your IP address to Google.

We may disclose data if a competent authority requires it by law.

5. How long we keep it

For as long as you have an account. If you delete it, we erase your account, habits and marks. Hosting backups may be kept for a limited time before they are removed.

6. Your rights

You can exercise your rights of access, rectification, cancellation and objection, and ask for a copy of your data or its deletion, at any time by writing to rayita@alanbazan.com.mx from the email you signed up with. We reply within 20 business days at most.

7. How to delete your account

Write to rayita@alanbazan.com.mx with the subject "Delete account" from your account email. We delete your account and data within 30 days at most and confirm by email.

8. Security

We use encrypted connections (HTTPS), store passwords as hashes and limit access to data. No system is completely secure, but we work to protect your information.

9. Children

Rayita is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data. If we find an account belonging to a child under that age, we delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The new version will be published here with its update date.

11. Contact

rayita@alanbazan.com.mx
